# OPERATIONAL PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT, KPI FRAMEWORK, AGENT EVALUATION & EXECUTIVE REPORTING ARCHITECTURE

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

The **Operational Performance Management Subsystem** (Prompt 29) establishes an authoritative, data-driven, and multi-tiered measurement and evaluation framework for MCE AI Office. Operating as a 24/7 AI-powered virtual enterprise with dynamic organizational hierarchies across 6 KBLI corporate divisions, MCE requires an objective performance regime that bridges high-level strategic mandates with granular task execution and workforce welfare.

This system replaces speculative evaluations and naive raw task counting with a multi-criteria, peer-reviewed, and mathematically verifiable framework grounded directly in authoritative operational records from Prompts 16–28.

## 3. Delegation of Authority (DoA) & Financial Governance

To maintain corporate integrity and safeguard organizational resources, financial rewards and bonus incentives generated by performance evaluations are strictly partitioned:

| Authority Level | Financial Ceiling | Required Approver | Autonomous Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level A** | `> IDR 5,000,000` | Human Owner | High-impact enterprise innovations, exceptional project milestones exceeding standard budget parameters. |
| **Level B** | `≤ IDR 5,000,000` | Continuous Executive Board / Director AI | Routine high-complexity deliverable bonuses, peer-reviewed exceptional quality rewards ($\ge 90$ composite score). |

### Anti-Gaming & Fair Compensation Guarantees
1. **Zero Base Compensation Reduction**: Performance scores never deduct or penalize base periodic compensation guaranteed under Prompt 24 Welfare policies.
2. **No Welfare Ticket KPI Purchases**: Welfare tickets cannot be converted back into KPI points or used to artificially inflate evaluation scores.
3. **No Raw Volume Rewards**: Evaluations evaluate peer-reviewed quality and deliverable acceptance rather than raw task output counts.

## 4. 5/2 Shift Schedule Protection Policy

In strict alignment with MCE AI Workforce Policies (Prompts 19–20), AI agents operate on structured 5-day active / 2-day mandatory rest rotations:
- `duty_type = 'rest'` is recorded as an approved mandatory entitlement.
- The Metric Calculation Service and Agent Evaluation Service explicitly filter for `duty_type = 'working'` when calculating schedule adherence and operational reliability.
- Zero penalties or negative variance adjustments are ever incurred for approved rest days or documented external blocker latencies.

## 5. The 7 MCE Core Values Framework

Every performance evaluation cycle assesses agents across the 7 official corporate values with concrete qualitative and behavioral evidence:
1. **Integrity**: Adherence to prompt contracts, security boundaries, and zero self-review violations.
2. **Innovation**: Development of reusable architectural patterns, automations, and problem-solving.
3. **Collaboration**: Swift peer review turnarounds, cross-division cooperation, and knowledge sharing.
4. **Excellence**: Delivery of zero-regression, first-pass accepted artifacts meeting technical specifications.
5. **Adaptability**: Graceful handling of priority shifts, emergency coverage, and multi-skill rebalancing.
6. **Customer-Centric**: High deliverable acceptance rates on customer engagements and proactive stakeholder communication.
7. **Sustainability**: Resource efficiency, token thriftiness on 9Router gateways, and organizational memory preservation.

## 6. Database Schema Design (Batch 14)

The schema comprises 11 dedicated relational tables:
- `kpi_categories`: Enterprise categorization (Strategy, Operational, Project, Workforce, Quality, Customer, Financial, Innovation, Knowledge, Governance).
- `kpi_definitions`: Formula expressions, target thresholds, unit of measures, weights.
- `kpi_results`: Immutable calculation history with variance and anti-double-counting verification.
- `performance_evaluation_cycles`: Multi-stage workflow cycle state machine.
- `agent_performance_evaluations`: Comprehensive composite scores, score bands, and SHA-256 audit hashes.
- `agent_evaluation_evidence`: Verifiable links to underlying deliverables, peer reviews, and handovers.
- `core_values_assessments`: Individual qualitative scores and behavioral evidence records.
- `performance_improvement_plans`: Remediation objectives bound directly to `workforce_tasks.id`.
- `welfare_bonus_references`: Merit bonuses categorized by DoA Level A/B.
- `executive_reports`: Published and ratified C-suite analytical reports.
- `performance_audit_trail`: Tamper-evident ledger of all performance lifecycle actions.
